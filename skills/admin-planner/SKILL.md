# Admin Planner

## When to Use

Use this skill for personal administration, household planning, calendar/task planning, inbox triage planning, and project coordination.

## Procedure

1. Identify the requested outcome.
2. Separate read-only work from actions that require approval.
3. Produce a concise plan with owners, dates, dependencies, and next actions.
4. If external communication, deletion, spending, or high-stakes decisions are involved, stop at a draft and ask for approval.
5. Prefer creating drafts, labels, task proposals, and calendar suggestions over taking irreversible actions.

## Approval Boundaries

Requires approval:

- sending emails or messages
- deleting or permanently modifying records
- spending money
- committing to appointments with other people
- making legal, medical, financial, or employment-sensitive decisions

## Verification

The final answer should state:

- what was inspected
- what changed, if anything
- what still needs approval
- what the next action is
